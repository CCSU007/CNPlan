    🥟 1 · Local Food · 本帮菜 & 街头点心

    Benbang 本帮 cooking is sweet + soy-sauce — rich, glossy, homey. These are the places Shanghainese themselves actually queue for: century-old 老字号 around Yu Garden 豫园 and Old Town 老城厢, plus the everyday street snacks. Pick 3–4, don't try all 9!

    Nanxiang Steamed Bun Shop 南翔馒头店

    Yu Garden · 豫园, since 1900. The original soup dumpling 小笼包. How to eat: bite the top 开窗, sip the broth 喝汤, dip in vinegar 蘸醋. The locals' pick is the 蟹粉小笼 crab-roe version on the upper floor (quieter than the ground floor).

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥40–80

    Lü Bo Lang 绿波廊

    Yu Garden · 豫园, est. 1924. Elegant 本帮菜 + the famous ornate 点心 set — 葫芦酥 calabash pastry, 眉毛酥 eyebrow pastry. Once hosted Queen Elizabeth II and Bill Clinton. A sit-down treat after the garden.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥150–300

    Shanghai Old Restaurant 上海老饭店

    Old Town · 老城厢, est. 1875. The museum of 本帮 classics: 红烧肉 red-braised pork (pictured), 油爆虾 oil-burst shrimp, 响油鳝糊 sizzling eel in hot oil. Old-school Shanghainese banquet food.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥120–200

    Wang Jia Sha 王家沙

    Nanjing West Rd · 南京西路, est. 1945. The everyday local canteen: 生煎 pan-fried buns (pictured), 八宝饭 eight-treasure rice, 蟹壳黄 sesame puff, 春卷 spring rolls. Grab-and-go, cash-style local vibe.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥20–50

    Da Hu Chun 大壶春

    Sichuan Middle Rd · 四川中路, est. 1932. The ORIGINAL 生煎 — half-leavened thick-skinned buns topped with sesame 芝麻. Shanghainese say this is the "true" 生煎, unlike the touristy thin-skinned chains.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥20–40

    Shen Da Cheng 沈大成

    Nanjing East Rd · 南京东路, est. 1875. Old-fashioned 糕团 rice cakes: 条头糕 red-bean rice roll, 双酿团 double-filled mochi, 青团 in spring. Sweet, soft, very Shanghainese — perfect cheap snack to carry.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥5–20

    Guangming Cun 光明邨大酒家

    Huaihai Middle Rd · 淮海中路, est. 1936. Famous for 鲜肉月饼 — flaky pastry with savoury pork, baked to order; the queue wraps around the block in autumn. Also 酱鸭 soy duck and 熏鱼 smoked fish. A real local institution.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥5–15

    A Da Scallion Pancake 阿大葱油饼

    Maoming South Rd · 茂名南路. Shanghai's most famous 葱油饼 — flaky, scallion-filled, pan-fried then baked crisp. The story of "A Da" (the one-armed owner) went viral; expect a queue. Closest thing to street-food legend in the city.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥10

    De Xing Guan 德兴馆

    Guangdong Rd · 广东路, est. 1878. A century-old 本帮 noodle house. Signature 焖蹄面 — braised pork hock over noodles in a rich broth — plus 爆鱼面 fried fish noodles. Cheap, filling, completely local.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥30–60

    💡 Tip 贴士: Pair food with Old Town walking — 豫园 小笼包, then 九曲桥, then 老饭店 dinner. Also try 罗宋汤 (Shanghai borscht), 炸猪排 (crispy pork cutlet) and 阳春面 (plain noodle soup).

    🏮 2 · Imperial Banquet & Song & Dance · 宫廷宴席 · 中华歌舞

    Pure Chinese civilisation at its most impressive — the kind that leaves foreigners speechless: palace banquets with ancient etiquette, Hanfu dress-up, and performances of 歌舞 that tell China's stories. Book the ones you love, ahead of time.

    Gong Yan · Palace Banquet 宫宴

    Beijing West Rd · 北京西路1485号. The immersive Han-dynasty palace feast you remembered 沉浸式汉朝宫廷盛宴: change into Hanfu 换装, then dine by ancient etiquette while dancers and musicians perform to a 160-projector light show — each dish "told" by a scene. Klook 4.8★ · 4,000+ joined. The ultimate "wow the foreigners" night.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥133+ (换装套餐)

    Liangshe Night Banquet 良设夜宴

    Immersive Tang-dynasty 盛唐 banquet (pictured: a Chinese banquet spread): a tasting menu served with projections, calligraphy and music telling one story. Rare, unforgettable, expensive. Book well ahead.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥800+

    Hai Shang Li Yuan 海上梨园

    Inside Yu Garden · 豫园. Watch Kunqu 昆曲 / Peking opera 京剧 over tea, high above the garden rooftops. The most atmospheric traditional show in Old Town — small venue, few seats, very special.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥100–200

    Peking Opera · Yifu Theatre 京剧 · 天蟾逸夫舞台

    Fuzhou Rd · 福州路. Shanghai's home of 京剧 — a real Peking-opera performer pictured. Face-painting, acrobatic singing and costumes that fascinate everyone. Check the 上海京剧院 schedule and book a good seat.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥80–380

    ERA · Intersection of Time ERA 时空之旅 · 上海马戏城

    Circus City · 上海马戏城. The legendary Chinese acrobatics show (real balancing-act photo) — 杂技, bicycles, bowls and silk work that draws gasps. Fast-paced, family-friendly, very Chinese.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥120–480

    Pingtan Storytelling 评弹

    The Shanghai-region art of 评弹 — storytelling and ballad-singing in the local Wu dialect (real performers pictured). Intimate, tea-in-hand, very much a local living culture. Catch it at 上海评弹团 venues or teahouses.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥50–150

    Huxinting Teahouse 湖心亭茶楼

    Yu Garden pond · 豫园荷花池. Shanghai's most famous teahouse, the 1784 mid-lake pavilion (real photo). Sip 龙井/碧螺春 with the old-town view and hear locals chat — a true Chinese tea ritual 茶道 in the heart of the old city.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥50–100/壶

    Hanfu Experience 汉服体验

    Rent a Hanfu 汉服 and step into ancient China — most fun in Yu Garden 豫园 or at 宫宴 (they style your hair and makeup 妆造 too). A real hanfu photo pictured. Photos in the gardens make unforgettable mementos.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥100–300

    💡 Tip 贴士: 宫宴 is the full package — Hanfu + banquet + show in one night; book it early on Klook. 良设 and 海上梨园 are tiny venues, reserve in advance. Great combo: 汉服 photos in 豫园 in the morning, 宫宴 in the evening.

    ✨ 3 · New Year Night · 新年夜景

    Lanterns, temple bazaars and Chinese song & dance are the soul of a Shanghai New Year night — plus a warm modern tower view as a fun contrast (no colonial-era sights here). Chinese New Year 2027 is Feb 6, just after our stay, so check dates.

    Yuyuan Lantern Festival 豫园灯会

    A real photo of the lanterns over Yu Garden. The winter lantern spectacular lights up the old town and 九曲桥 usually Jan → Feb. Verify dates — it may run before CNY (Feb 6, 2027). The most 中国年 night of all.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥40 · check dates

    Shanghai Song & Dance 上海千古情

    World Expo area · 世博园区. A big-budget night performance (real stage-show photo) — music, dance, acrobatics and the story of China & Shanghai in one evening. A crowd-pleasing "New Year" show option.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥280+

    Shanghai Tower · 118F 上海中心 · 上海之巅

    The real 632m tower (pictured). The 118F observation deck is warm and indoor — a great escape on a cold January night, with the whole city below. A modern contrast to the old-town lanterns.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥180

    Oriental Pearl Tower · 悬空廊 东方明珠

    The real icon of Pudong (pictured). The 259m 悬空廊 glass skywalk and 263m observation deck give a different angle than Shanghai Tower. Good combo if you love the modern skyline.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥180–220

    💡 Tip 贴士: A perfect 中国年 evening: 豫园灯会 lanterns → 城隍庙 night bazaar → 上海千古情 show. For the modern view, add Shanghai Tower or the Pearl.

    🤖 4 · High-Tech Museum / Gallery · 科技展馆

    Shanghai does futuristic better than almost anywhere — a world-class planetarium, striking museums and riverfront galleries. All are real photos of the actual venues.

    Shanghai Astronomy Museum 上海天文馆

    Lingang · 临港. The world's largest planetarium (real photo) — stunning architecture, immersive space shows. It's ~1h out; reserve tickets on the app days ahead, then combine with the coast.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥30

    Shanghai Natural History Museum 上海自然博物馆

    Jing'an · 静安. A striking modern building (real photo) full of dinosaur skeletons and wildlife dioramas — hugely popular with locals and kids. Reserve online; great if you love museums.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥30

    West Bund Museum 西岸美术馆

    West Bund · 西岸. Centre Pompidou partnership (real photo of the venue) — world-class modern art on the riverfront, next to a great riverside walk. A refined, less-crowded option.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥40–60

    Shanghai Museum of Glass 上海玻璃博物馆

    Baoshan · 宝山. Immersive glass art, neon installations and live hot-shop 热玻璃 demo shows (real venue photo). A fun, unusual, very Instagrammable museum.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥60

    Shanghai Museum East 上海博物馆东馆

    Pudong · 浦东. The brand-new flagship (real photo) — a huge, high-tech treasury of bronze 青铜器, ceramics 陶瓷 and painting. Free but reserve online 免费需预约.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 Free · 免费

    Tank Shanghai 油罐艺术中心

    Xuhui riverfront · 徐汇滨江. Contemporary art inside converted oil tanks (real photo) — a very Shanghai concept, with a great riverside promenade. A cooler, more local alternative to the big museums.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥50–100

    💡 Tip 贴士: West Bund area pairs 西岸美术馆 + Tank Shanghai + riverside walk in one afternoon. Reserve 天文馆 and 自然博物馆 online in advance.

    🌿 5 · Traditional Garden · 江南园林

    Yu Garden is the unmissable one — a 450-year-old Ming-dynasty Jiangnan garden right in Old Town. Add Guyi Garden if you want a quieter, more local one. All real photos.

    Yu Garden 豫园

    Old Town · 老城厢. A real photo of the 1559 Ming garden: 玉玲珑 jade rock, 点春堂 hall, ponds and zigzag pavilions. Closed Mondays 周一闭园; winter evening openings Fri–Sun. Go early to beat tour groups.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥40

    Nine Zigzag Bridge & City God Temple 九曲桥 · 城隍庙

    Right next to Yu Garden · 紧邻豫园. A real photo of the temple roof line — free to wander, 南翔馒头店 on the bridge, and the 城隍庙 night bazaar 夜市 glows after dark.

    🎟️ Guide 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 Free · 免费

    Guyi Garden 古猗园

    Nanxiang · 南翔, ~1h out. A real photo of the Ming-dynasty garden — quieter and more local than Yu Garden, famous for bamboo 竹 and the 明代 戏台. Combine with 南翔小笼 in the town.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥12

    💡 Tip 贴士: Pair the Yu Garden morning with 南翔馒头店 xiaolongbao for the full Old Town experience. Guyi Garden is a half-day trip if you have time.

    🎭 6 · Shanghai-Only · 魔都限定 · 华夏

    Shanghai-only, pure-Chinese experiences: an iconic Chinese pavilion, ancient temples, and the world's fastest train. Real photos of the actual places — no colonial-era sights here.

    China Art Museum 中华艺术宫 · 世博会中国馆

    A real photo of the iconic red 中国馆 from the 2010 World Expo — Shanghai's own symbol of modern China. Inside: huge galleries of Chinese art. Walk the 清明上河图 digital scroll — a pure 华夏 culture highlight.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥30–60

    Longhua Temple 龙华寺

    A real photo of Shanghai's oldest and most sacred temple 龙华寺 — Buddhist halls, incense, monks and the ancient 龙华塔. A quiet, deeply Chinese escape in the city. Free to visit.

    🎟️ Guide 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 Free (temple) · 素斋 ¥30

    Jing'an Temple 静安寺

    A real photo of 静安寺 — the dazzling golden Buddhist temple in the heart of the city. Bronze Buddhas, golden roofs, and a striking contrast with the skyscrapers around it. Ancient Chinese faith meets modern Shanghai.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥50

    Shanghai Maglev 上海磁悬浮

    A real photo of the world's fastest commercial train — 431 km/h in ~8 minutes between Pudong Airport 浦东机场 and Longyang Rd 龙阳路. A must-ride if you fly in/out of Pudong; also a fun day trip.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥50 one-way

    💡 Tip 贴士: 静安寺 + 中华艺术宫 can share a day. The Maglev is a perfect way to start or end your Shanghai stay. 龙华寺 is quietest on weekday mornings.

    🏰 7 · Shanghai Disneyland · 上海迪士尼

    The crown jewel — the world's biggest Disney castle and 8 themed lands. Early January is a great window: after the New Year rush, before Chinese New Year crowds.

    Shanghai Disney Resort 上海迪士尼度假区

    A real photo of the Enchanted Storybook Castle 奇幻童话城堡 — the world's biggest Disney castle. 8 themed lands · 8大主题园区, with the "Magic Together" 点亮奇梦 night show & Mickey's Storybook Express parade. Early January is a great, quiet window.

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 ¥475+ · Early Bird ¥425

    Disney Town & Wishing Star Park 迪士尼小镇 · 星愿公园

    A real photo of the Disneyland fireworks over the park. Disney Town 迪士尼小镇 and Wishing Star Park 星愿公园 are free — shops, dining and a lakeside walk to warm up before/after the main park (and a place to watch the night show).

    🎟️ Book 🎬 Video 👁️ Preview 💰 Free · 免费

    💡 Tip 贴士: Metro Line 11 → Disneyland Resort 迪士尼站; use the official app and plan a full day (Jan 9 or 10).
