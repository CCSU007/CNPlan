💰 Money · 货币

  • Almost everything is paid with Alipay 支付宝 / WeChat Pay 微信支付 — even street stalls.
  • Carry ~¥200–500 cash as backup; ATMs (银联) at airports & banks.
  • No tipping expected in China.

📱 SIM / eSIM · 上网

  • eSIM: Airalo / Holafly / Saily — 30-day China data ~¥150–220, install before you fly.
  • Or buy a local SIM (中国移动 / 联通) at the airport with your passport.

🌐 VPN · 翻墙

  • Google / WhatsApp / Instagram / Facebook are blocked in China.
  • Install a reliable VPN (ExpressVPN, NordVPN…) before arriving and keep it running.

🚇 Apps · 应用

  • Metro大都会 — subway QR tickets.
  • 高德地图 Amap — GPS & navigation.
  • 滴滴 DiDi — rides (English UI) · 12306 — trains · Trip.com — English bookings.

🛂 Visa & Entry · 签证

  • NZ passport: 15-day visa-free transit — verify current rules before flying.
  • Carry hotel addresses + return ticket (printed helps).

🆘 Emergency · 紧急

  • Police 110 · Fire 119 · Ambulance 120 · Train booking 12306.
  • English help at airports and major hotels.
