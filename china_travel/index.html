Timeline

Your Journey Step by Step

Every stop has a purpose. Here is the recommended pacing for each destination.

Beijing · 4 nights

12 May – 16 May · Spring weather (18–28°C)

Kick off with China's imperial heart: the Forbidden City, Great Wall, Temple of Heaven, and legendary Peking duck. Great spring weather for outdoor sightseeing.

Dalian · 3 nights

16 May – 19 May · Mild coastal spring (15–22°C)

A relaxing seaside pause. Stroll Xinghai Square, drive the Binhai Road, and enjoy fresh seafood. A nice contrast after busy Beijing.

Shanghai · 5 nights

19 May – 24 May · Warm late spring (20–28°C)

Modern China at its finest. The Bund, Huangpu River cruise, Yu Garden, Nanjing Road shopping, and world-class dining. Plenty to explore.

Suzhou · 2 nights

24 May – 26 May · Warm spring (20–27°C)

Only 30 min from Shanghai by train. Famous for classical gardens and ancient canals. A calm, beautiful contrast to the big cities.

Chongqing · 4 nights

26 May – 30 May · Warm to hot (22–30°C)

A dramatic mountain city where the Yangtze meets the Jialing River. Ride the cable car, enjoy the night skyline, and eat the famous Chongqing hotpot.

Chengdu · 6 nights

30 May – 5 Jun · Warm with some rain (22–28°C)

The panda capital. Visit the Giant Panda Breeding Base, explore tea houses on Jinli Ancient Street, and feast on Sichuan hotpot and street food.

Wangxian Valley · 3 nights

5 Jun – 8 Jun · Warm & scenic (22–30°C)

A nature escape with spectacular valley views, hiking trails, and fresh mountain air. Perfect for photography and a slower pace after city energy.

✈️ Thailand · 10 nights

24 Dec – 2 Jan · Warm & tropical (25–32°C)

Escape the China winter for sun, beaches, temples, and incredible food. A perfect mid-trip recharge before your final stop.

Shenzhen · 7 nights

3 Jan – 10 Jan · Mild winter (15–22°C)

End your journey in a warm, modern city. Visit Window of the World, OCT Harbour, relax in coastal parks, and enjoy excellent seafood and shopping.

Travel Info

Transport, Weather & EFT

Everything you need to know about moving between cities and what to expect from the climate.

✈️ Recommended Transport

  • Long distance: Flights (Beijing→Dalian, Dalian→Shanghai, Shanghai→Chongqing, Chengdu→Shenzhen)
  • Short hops: High-speed trains (Shanghai↔Suzhou 30min, Chongqing↔Chengdu ~3h)
  • Within cities: Metro, Didi (ride-hailing), or taxis — all affordable and reliable

⏱️ Estimated Travel Time (EFT)

  • Beijing → Dalian: 2–3h by flight
  • Dalian → Shanghai: 2h by flight
  • Shanghai → Suzhou: 30–45 min by high-speed train
  • Suzhou → Chongqing: 2.5h by flight
  • Chongqing → Chengdu: ~3h by high-speed train
  • Chengdu → Wangxian Valley: ~4h by car/bus
  • Chengdu → Shenzhen: 2.5h by flight
  • Shenzhen → Thailand: 3–4h by flight

💡 Pro Tips

  • Book flights 2–4 weeks ahead for best prices
  • Download Alipay and WeChat Pay before arrival
  • Get a VPN set up before arriving in China
  • Carry a power bank — phone battery is essential for maps
  • Learn basic Chinese phrases (Didi = taxi app)

Budget

Cost Breakdown

Estimated mid-range budget per person. Actual costs vary by season and booking time.

✈️ Transport

$350–$700 total

Includes all flights between cities plus high-speed trains. Booking early saves a lot.

🏨 Hotels (mid-range)

$45–$90 / night

Beijing, Shanghai & Shenzhen are pricier ($60–90). Smaller cities like Suzhou, Chengdu & Dalian are more affordable ($40–60).

🍜 Food & Activities

$20–$40 / day

Street food and local restaurants are cheap ($5–15). Nice dinners and attraction tickets add to the budget.

🚕 City Transport

$5–$15 / day

Metro is very affordable. Didi rides start around $3–$8 depending on distance.

🎟️ Attractions

$10–$40 / site

Major sites like Forbidden City (~$8), Great Wall (~$10), Window of the World (~$20). Budget ~$100–$200 for all.

📱 Extras

$50–$150 total

VPN, SIM card / eSIM, travel insurance, laundry, souvenirs.

💰 Estimated Total (per person)

$2,200 – $4,000

For the entire China portion (~44 nights). The Thailand trip is additional.

Mid-Trip Escape

🇹🇭 Thailand · 24 Dec – 2 Jan

A well-timed winter break before your final stop in Shenzhen.

✈️ Getting There & Back

From Wangxian Valley / Chengdu → Fly to Bangkok (or Chiang Mai / Phuket). A direct flight from Chengdu takes about 3 hours.

Back to China → Fly from Bangkok to Shenzhen (~3h). Easy connection for your final stop.

🏖️ Recommended Thailand Route

  • Bangkok (3 nights) — Temples, street food, markets
  • Chiang Mai (3 nights) — Mountains, night bazaar, elephant sanctuaries
  • Phuket / Krabi (4 nights) — Beaches, islands, snorkeling
